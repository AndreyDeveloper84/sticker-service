# MVP Scope v0.2

Status: DRAFT

MVP launches in two channels from day one: Telegram Bot and MAX Bot. Both use one backend and one business logic.

Products in MVP:
- P01 Personal Sticker Pack
- P02 Personal Avatar

Core flow:
Telegram/MAX → product → style → photos → checkout → payment → preview → approval/revision → production → QC → delivery.

Human-in-the-loop remains part of MVP through one shared Production Console.

MVP must prove paid demand, preview approval rate, revision rate, AI cost per order, Manual Minutes per Order, and whether order volume can grow without linear growth in human work.
